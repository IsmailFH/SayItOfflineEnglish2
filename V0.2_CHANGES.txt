SayIt English v0.2

FIXED FROM v0.1
- Removed the fixed 15-question Grammar list.
- Added generated Grammar sessions (10 questions/session) across 15 skills.
- Grammar weakness tracking now affects what gets reviewed next.
- Vocabulary bank expanded from 24 to 104 A2-B1 words.
- Vocabulary is now interactive: meaning choice, context choice, and typed recall.
- Spelling randomizes from the full vocabulary bank.
- Dictation expanded to 40 randomized sentences.
- Writing Coach output is structured and short: score, correction, max 4 concrete errors, natural version.
- Improve Sentence output is structured: corrected, natural, stronger, vocabulary replacements.
- Removed the tiny Qwen LLM from Writing/Improve because it was slow and hallucinated mixed Arabic/English output.
- Writing/Improve now run instantly with a deterministic local English engine.
- Speaking still uses Whisper Tiny English locally; only ~75 MB one-time model download is needed.
- Progress now tracks Grammar, Vocabulary, Spelling, Dictation, Speaking and Writing separately.
